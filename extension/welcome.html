StreamSnag is installed

Three short steps and you're saving videos.

  1. 1

    Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of the browser, then the pin next to StreamSnag. The icon shows a number when a page has videos.

  2. 2

    Open a page and press play

    Many players only load the video once it starts. Then click the StreamSnag icon: the video you're watching is at the top with one Download button, and everything else on the page is listed below it.

  3. 3

    Add the helper optional, recommended

    A small program on your computer that gives you one finished MP4 (video and audio merged), whole playlists, saving into any folder, and a check of which videos you already have.

    • Windows: in the StreamSnag folder you downloaded, double-click Set up StreamSnag helper.cmd.
    • macOS / Linux: run sh setup.sh in that folder.
    • Restart the browser, then press Check helper.

Good to know

  • Nothing leaves your computer except the downloads themselves. No accounts, no tracking. Privacy details
  • AI features are off until you turn them on in Settings.
  • Protected (DRM) videos can't be downloaded. StreamSnag tells you when a video is protected.
