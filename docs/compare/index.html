What StreamSnag does not do.

We do not claim StreamSnag does everything Video DownloadHelper does. From the table above:

  • It does not run in Firefox.
  • It records live streams only as they play, and some kinds of live broadcast it cannot record by itself.
  • Some of its features need a helper program installed on your computer.
  • It has not been tried on anything like as many sites, over anything like as many years. It is built for course and lecture pages.
  • Its refund window is 7 days, which is shorter.

What costs money, in each.

In Video DownloadHelper, the thing you pay for is more downloading: the free version limits streams, and Premium removes the limit. In StreamSnag, downloading is never limited. What you pay for is the study side, which a downloader does not have: transcription on your own computer, the reading edition, the study page, the study pack and review cards.

If all you want is to save lecture videos, StreamSnag is free for that and you never need Pro.

What the AI parts cost

And compared with online study-note tools.

A downloader is one thing people compare StreamSnag with. The other is the kind of website or app that turns a recording into notes. This part describes that kind in general and names no product and no price of theirs: their plans differ and change, so check the one you are considering. We read the public plan pages of a few of them on 11 November 2026, and what follows is what those had in common.

How this was checked.

People who look for a way to save lecture videos usually find Video DownloadHelper first. It is a browser extension for saving web video, and by its own account it has been “used by millions for almost 20 years”. StreamSnag is new, and it is built for one job: saving lectures and studying from them.
