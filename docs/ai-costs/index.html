StreamSnag/What the AI costs

AI costs are not included in the price.

Nothing extra on a Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already have; a few cents to about US$1.50 a lecture with a pay-as-you-go key; never paid to StreamSnag.

Saving, transcription, the reading edition, the study page and reviewing cards use no AI account at all.

What a study pack cost on our own runs.

With a pay-as-you-go key (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter) you are billed for what the run uses: the table shows what that came to.

Measured in November 2026: the 64-minute pack took about 30 AI requests. Prices are set by the AI providers and change. A longer lecture costs more, and your lectures and settings will give different numbers.

Your own AI account, never ours.

StreamSnag has no servers, so it cannot run an AI for you. The study pack, and the optional tidying of a reading edition, are written by an AI account that is your own. What you spend there, if anything, is between you and that provider. It is never paid to StreamSnag, which adds no limit of its own and takes no share.

A Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already have, or a key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek or OpenRouter. You choose one in Settings, and AI stays off until you do.

You see what is sent, before it is sent.

Before every AI run you see what will be sent, to which provider, and an estimate of how much AI use it takes. An estimate is not a guarantee, and your provider may have limits of its own.

What goes is a lecture's transcript and the text on its slides, with the speaker notes when the slides are a PowerPoint file. Slide pictures and the video are never sent. The smaller AI helpers in the popup (Ask your AI to find it, Summarize, Ask AI why) each show what they would send before they send it. It is sent from your computer, because StreamSnag has no servers.

If your allowance runs out.

With a Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already have, there is no extra charge. Each run counts toward your plan's limits, and a plan can run out part-way through a busy day. Google Gemini has a free daily allowance and then charges your Google account. We have not tested whether a whole lecture fits in it, and a free allowance can run out part-way.

The run stops and StreamSnag tells you why. Nothing is charged by us, and what you had already saved or made stays where it is. You can run it again later, or choose another AI account in Settings. Free allowances are the usual cause: they can do part of a study pack and run out part-way.

What the second sample pack took.

The statistics pack was 20 AI requests in 1 minute 31 seconds and cost US$0.80 on Claude Sonnet. Of 65 statements pulled from the lecture, 61 passed the quote check. Of 17 flashcards written, 9 were kept.
