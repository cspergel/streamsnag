StreamSnag/Preview

The StreamSnag preview

You were sent this page to try StreamSnag before it launches. It takes about five minutes to set up, on a computer with Chrome, Edge or Brave.

This is the whole of StreamSnag: the free video downloader, and the study tools of Pro. The downloader needs nothing else. For Pro, your first lecture is free from start to finish, and the person who sent you this page may give you a key for the rest.

Set it up

  1. Download the extension

    Download StreamSnag 1.7.0 1.5 MB

    Unzip it into a folder you will keep, such as Documents, not Downloads: the browser runs it from that folder, and deleting the folder removes StreamSnag.

  2. Add it to your browser

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (in Edge, edge://extensions). Turn on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the folder you unzipped: the one with manifest.json in it. Then pin StreamSnag to the toolbar with the puzzle-piece button.

    The browser may remind you that developer-mode extensions are on. That is expected for a preview: the store version will not need it.

  3. Add the helper (Windows, recommended)

    Download the helper for Windows 33 MB

    Unzip it and double-click Set up StreamSnag helper.cmd. It brings everything it needs, offers to install ffmpeg and needs no admin rights. Then restart the browser, open StreamSnag's Settings, choose Local helper and press Check helper.

    The helper joins a video's picture and sound into one file, saves into any folder, and runs Pro's transcription on your computer. Windows may ask whether to run a file from an unknown publisher the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac the downloader works without it; a Mac helper comes later.

  4. Pro: paste your key

    If you were given a key, open StreamSnag's Settings, choose Pro and paste it. Without one, your first lecture through the study tools is free from start to finish: transcript, reading edition and study pack.

Then try it

Open a page with a video you are allowed to keep, press play, and click the StreamSnag icon. The video is at the top with one Download button. The downloader page and the guides show the rest.

Good to know

  • A new preview: unzip it over the same folder, replacing the files, then press the round arrow on StreamSnag's card in chrome://extensions. Your settings and your desk stay.
  • To remove it: press Remove on its card in chrome://extensions. The helper is in Windows Settings, under Apps, Installed apps, StreamSnag helper.
  • Found something wrong? Tell the person who sent you this page, or write to support@streamsnag.app with the address of the page and what happened.
