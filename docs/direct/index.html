Install StreamSnag from this site

Most people should get StreamSnag from the Chrome Web Store: one click, and it keeps itself up to date. If you would rather install it yourself, here it is. It takes about five minutes, on a computer with Chrome, Edge or Brave.

It is the same StreamSnag: the free video downloader, and the study tools of Pro. Your first lecture through Pro is free from start to finish, and a Pro key works here just as it does in the store version.

Set it up

  1. Download the extension

    Download StreamSnag 1.7.2 1.5 MB

    Unzip it into a folder you will keep, such as Documents, not Downloads: the browser runs it from that folder, and deleting the folder removes StreamSnag.

  2. Add it to your browser

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar (in Edge, edge://extensions). Turn on Developer mode, press Load unpacked and choose the folder you unzipped: the one with manifest.json in it. Then pin StreamSnag to the toolbar with the puzzle-piece button.

    The browser may remind you that developer-mode extensions are on. That is expected for an extension installed this way.

  3. Add the helper (Windows, recommended)

    Download the helper for Windows 33 MB

    Unzip it and double-click Set up StreamSnag helper.cmd. It brings everything it needs, offers to install ffmpeg and needs no admin rights. Then restart the browser, open StreamSnag's Settings, choose Local helper and press Check helper.

    The helper joins a video's picture and sound into one file, saves into any folder, and runs Pro's transcription on your computer. Windows may ask whether to run a file from an unknown publisher the first time: choose More info, then Run anyway (no need to turn off SmartScreen or your antivirus). On a Mac the downloader works without it; a Mac helper comes later.

  4. Pro (optional)

    Try your first lecture through the study tools for free. For the rest, get a Pro key, then open StreamSnag's Settings, choose Pro and paste it.

Using it

Open a page with a video you are allowed to keep, press play, and click the StreamSnag icon. The video is at the top with one Download button. To try it first, use the sample lecture. The downloader page and the guides show the rest.

Good to know

  • Updates are by hand. An extension installed this way does not update itself. For a new version, come back to this page, unzip it over the same folder, replacing the files, then press the round arrow on StreamSnag's card in chrome://extensions. Your settings and your desk stay.
  • One or the other. If you have the store version, remove it first: the two cannot be installed side by side.
  • Check the downloads, if you like: both files' SHA-256 are in SHA256SUMS.txt. In PowerShell, in their folder: Get-FileHash .\streamsnag-1.7.2.zip, and compare the long number with that file's line.
  • To remove it: press Remove on its card in chrome://extensions. The helper is in Windows Settings, under Apps, Installed apps, StreamSnag helper.
  • Something wrong? Write to support@streamsnag.app with the address of the page and what happened.
